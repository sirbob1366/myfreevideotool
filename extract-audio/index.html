Convert video to MP3 — without the upload marathon

Need the music from a recording, a podcast that only exists as video, or a lecture you'd rather listen to on a run? Drop the file and the audio track is extracted right in your browser. Because nothing is uploaded, even a two-hour video starts processing instantly — and extraction is fast, since only the audio is touched.

MP3, WAV or M4A?

  • MP3 — universal. Pick a bitrate: 192 kbps is transparent for most listeners; 96 kbps is plenty for speech.
  • WAV — lossless PCM, ideal if you'll edit the audio in another tool afterwards. Large files.
  • M4A (fast copy) — most MP4 videos already carry AAC audio; this mode copies that track bit-for-bit without re-encoding. It's the fastest option and loses literally nothing.

Common uses

  • Turning recorded webinars and lectures into listenable audio.
  • Grabbing your own published video's soundtrack when the project file is long gone.
  • Pulling interview audio out of camera footage for a podcast edit.

As always on this site: no upload, no watermark, no account — and your file is gone from memory the moment you close the tab.

FAQ

How do I convert a video to MP3?

Drop the video, pick MP3 and a bitrate, click Extract. The audio downloads in seconds — no upload, no account.

Which output should I pick — MP3, WAV or M4A?

MP3 for universal playback; WAV for lossless editing; M4A fast-copy for MP4 sources (bit-for-bit original audio, fastest).

Does extraction reduce audio quality?

WAV is lossless, M4A copy keeps the original exactly, and MP3 at 192 kbps+ is transparent for most listeners.

Is there a length or size limit?

No fixed limit — it runs on your device. Feature-length videos work; over 2 GB you get a heads-up, not a block.
