Questions, answered

Is my video really never uploaded?

Really never. The editor and every tool run on WebAssembly, Web Audio and WebGL inside your browser tab. Open your network inspector while editing — you'll see no video data leaving. The engine itself downloads once (~32 MB) and is cached; your files stay on your machine.

Where's the catch — watermark, file cap, account?

None of the three, ever. There's no server processing to ration, so there's nothing to paywall. The site is ad-supported.

How big a file can I edit?

There's no imposed limit — capacity is your device's memory. Multi-gigabyte files work; above 2 GB you get a friendly heads-up, not a block.

Why does export take as long as the video?

Honest answer: the editor composites every frame in real time on your device, so a 3-minute project exports in about 3 minutes. Single-tool operations like lossless trimming or muting skip this entirely and finish in seconds.

Does it work on phones and iPads?

Yes — the tools and editor work on iOS Safari and Android Chrome, with touch timeline controls. Heavy exports are faster on a laptop, but nothing is desktop-only.
