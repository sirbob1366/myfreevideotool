Watermark your video — without getting watermarked

There's a grim joke in this category: most "free" watermark tools stamp their own watermark on your video unless you pay. We find that absurd. Here you add your logo (transparent PNG) or a text mark, place it with presets or by dragging it directly on the preview, set the opacity, and export. Nothing else is ever added to your footage.

Logo vs. text

A PNG with transparency preserves exact branding — scale it with the width control (15–25% of video width works for most corner logos). A text mark is quicker for a handle, name or URL, rendered with a subtle shadow for legibility on any footage.

Placement strategy

  • Bottom-right — the convention; least intrusive, expected by viewers.
  • Center at low opacity — for preview copies you don't want cropped or stolen: a centered mark can't be cut off.
  • Match the content — drag the mark away from where the action lives; the preview shows exactly how it sits.

FAQ

Should I use a PNG or text watermark?

PNG with transparency for logos and exact branding; text for a quick handle or URL. Both support opacity and free placement.

What opacity should a watermark be?

40–60% is the sweet spot — attributable but watchable. Go higher for client preview copies.

Where should I place the watermark?

Bottom-right is conventional; center at low opacity resists cropping for anti-theft protection.

Is my video uploaded to watermark it?

No — rendered in your browser. And we never add our own mark to anything.
