The meme workflow, minus the friction

Drop a clip and the editor opens with two text layers already on the canvas — TOP TEXT and BOTTOM TEXT in the bold outlined style every meme uses, sized for readability, positioned where memes expect them. Click each one, type your line, and you're 90% done. Because they're real layers, you can also drag them anywhere, recolor them, or give them entrance animations.

Timing is the punchline

Great video memes land text at the right beat. Each caption is a clip on the timeline: drag its edges so the setup appears first and the payoff lands exactly on the reaction frame. Add a pop-in animation for extra punch.

Square it, caption it, ship it

Switch the canvas to 1:1 or 9:16 from the editor's preset menu for feed-native framing, and export as MP4 for maximum compatibility — or take the result to Video to GIF when the joke belongs in a group chat. Either way the file is clean: no watermark, no logo, no "made with" bug. It's your meme.

FAQ

How do I make a video meme?

Drop your clip — TOP/BOTTOM text layers are pre-placed in the classic style. Type, drag if needed, export. Under a minute.

Will there be a watermark on my meme?

Never. Exports are always clean.

Can the text appear only at certain moments?

Yes — each caption is a timeline clip; drag its edges to time it, with optional fade/pop animations.

Is my video uploaded to make the meme?

No — the editor runs entirely in your browser.
